### About

I work on federated and decentralized machine learning, with an interest in efficient communication and reinforcement learning. My research explores how distributed agents can learn collaboratively, with applications in intelligent transportation.

This profile brings together my research implementations, experiment resources, and learning projects.

### Research interests

- **Federated learning:** collaborative training across distributed data sources.
- **Decentralized learning:** communication-efficient model exchange and cooperation.
- **Reinforcement learning:** multi-agent decision-making and traffic simulation.
